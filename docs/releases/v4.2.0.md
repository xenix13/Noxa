## Noxa 4.2.0

A fix release for the Android TV app. Update the app on your TV to get these fixes: open *Downloader*, type `<your-server>/tv` and install it over the current app. Nothing needs to be uninstalled, and you stay signed in.

### Android TV app

- **"Who's watching?" after playback:** after a movie or an episode, or when you left the player, the app sometimes went back to the profile choice. That happened when Android restarted the app to free memory during playback. If you used the app in the last 30 minutes, it now goes straight back to your profile. When you quit the app on purpose, it still asks at the next launch.
- **Automatic quality on slow TVs:** the short speed test made at the start of a video could read far below the real connection speed on a slow TV, so the server converted videos for nothing. The app now also uses the speed the player itself sees while downloading the video, and keeps the higher of the two. If playback stalls, quality still goes down as before.
